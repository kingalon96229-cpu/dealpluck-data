Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

DealPluck ("the app", "we") is an independent mobile app that lists deals and promo codes from well-known brands in the United States. This policy explains what information is used when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

  • Favorites: the deals you save are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.
  • Deals list: the app downloads the list of deals from a public file hosted on GitHub. Like any website, GitHub may receive technical data such as your IP address to deliver the file.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads.

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • On iOS, personalized ads based on tracking are only used if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings, or reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Links to brands and other websites

When you tap "Open" on a deal, you leave DealPluck and go to the brand's own website or app. Their privacy policies apply there. Some links may become affiliate links in the future: if you buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We will always disclose this in the app.

4. How we use information

Information is used only to run the app, show ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Children

DealPluck is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example California under the CCPA/CPRA, or the EU under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, delete or limit the use of your personal information, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, most requests can be handled by uninstalling the app, resetting your advertising ID, or contacting Google. For any request, email us and we will help.

7. Data retention and deletion

Favorites stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. We do not keep a copy.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
